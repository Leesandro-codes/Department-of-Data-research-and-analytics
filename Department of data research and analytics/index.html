Explore Our department

Explore our website to learn more about our courses, activities, and the services we offer. We welcome students who are interested in learning and growing in the field of data and technology

Who we are

We're the department of data technology and analysis focuses on helping students understand how data is collected stored managed and used. We provide students with practical knowledge and skills that can be used in the technology and business world

What we do

Our department teaches students how to work with databases, data analysis tools, and computer systems. We encourage students to solve problems, work with data and use technology to make better decisions.

Our Goal

Our goal is to prepare students for careers in data technology and analysis. We aim to give students the skills, knowledge and confidence they need to work with daata and technology in the real world.
